Paste CSV data into the text field below to generate product information graphics (1400 × 980 pixels) in bulk. Adjust global text sizing and corner radius if needed, then export all images as a ZIP archive or download/copy them individually.

### CSV Structure Requirements

Your CSV file should be formatted with the following columns:

- **`SKU`** (Column 1): Product item code used for badge display and export filename.
- **`Title`** (Column 2): Main headline displayed at the top of the information graphic.
- **`Bullets`** (Column 3): Bullet point text. Separate multiple bullet points using a pipe character (`|`), semicolon (`;`), or line breaks.

> **Note:** Headers are automatically detected. Encapsulate strings containing commas in quotation marks. You can also download our [example CSV template](/product-review-assets/product-information-template.csv).

#### Example CSV Format

```csv
SKU,Title,Bullets
HAL-DIAG-01,"Diagnostic Check","Quickly identify the cause of warning lights|Peace of mind that your vehicle is in safe condition|Receive expert advice and recommendations from technicians"
HAL-AIRCON-02,"Air Con Service","Full system diagnostic & pressure test|Recharge refrigerant gas and lubricant|Eliminate bad odours and bacteria"
HAL-BATT-03,"Battery Health Check","Free 5-minute comprehensive battery test|Printout results provided on completion|No appointment required – visit any store"
```
